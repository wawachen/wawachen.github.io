Jingyu Chen

Researcher · Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences

I am a researcher at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences. I completed my Ph.D. in Robotics at the University of Sheffield, supervised by Dr. John Oyekan and Prof. Lyudmila Mihaylova.

My research focuses on robotics, world model and reinforcement learning.

  • Interactive world models for long-horizon tasks
  • Embodied navigation and obstacle-avoidance models for unmanned aerial vehicles
  • Fast point-cloud occupancy generation for large-scale scenes

Before my Ph.D., I received an M.Eng. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the University of Leicester and an M.Sc. in Robotics from the University of Sheffield.
